This policy explains what information DailyPulse collects, how Condor LLC uses it, and the choices you have. It covers only the DailyPulse app, not Condor's website or consulting services. Last updated October 9, 2026.

## The short version

- We collect only what DailyPulse needs to send check-in reminders and tell your circle how they went.
- Your check-ins, photos, and replies are seen only by the people in your circle.
- We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
- You can delete your account, and its information, at any time from My Circle.

## What we collect

| Information | From whom | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email address | Organizers | To sign in with a one-time code, and to send account and billing notices |
| Display name | Everyone | So people in the circle know who's who |
| Circle membership and role | Everyone | To know who checks in, who looks out for them, and who manages the circle |
| Check-in settings: times, time zone, reminders, quick replies, a note | Organizers | To send reminders at the right time |
| Check-ins: when, how (tap or photo), and any quick reply | The person checking in | To show the circle and alert them if one is missed |
| Photos taken for a check-in | The person checking in | To show the circle |
| Notification token and phone type (iPhone or Android) | Everyone | To send notifications to the right phone |
| Subscription status: plan, store, and end date | Apple or Google, through RevenueCat | To know whether a circle is active. We never see card or bank details. |
| Sign-in records, such as time and IP address | Everyone | To keep accounts secure and fix problems |

We don't collect your location, contacts, or health information, and we don't use the camera or photo library except when you take a check-in photo. People who check in can use DailyPulse without an email address.

## How we use it

We use your information to run, secure, support, and improve DailyPulse, and to follow the law. That means sending reminders and alerts, showing check-ins to your circle, keeping circles to their size and subscription, answering your questions, and fixing problems.

**We never:**

- sell or rent your information;
- use it for advertising, or share it with advertisers or data brokers;
- track you across other companies' apps or websites;
- use your photos or check-ins to train AI models.

## Who sees your information

**Your circle.** Active members of a circle see each other's names, the check-in settings, check-ins, quick replies, and photos. People waiting to be approved see only the circle's name. Someone removed from a circle stops seeing anything new.

**Companies that help us run DailyPulse.** They handle information only on our behalf and under contracts that limit how they use it.

| Company | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) | Stores our database and photos, and handles sign-in |
| [Expo](https://expo.dev/privacy) | Delivers notifications to phones |
| Apple and Google | Deliver notifications, and handle purchases under their own privacy policies |
| [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) | Keeps track of subscriptions |
| Google Workspace | Sends sign-in code emails |

**When the law requires it.** We may share information to follow a valid legal request, or to protect someone's safety. If Condor or DailyPulse is sold or reorganized, your information may pass to the new owner under this policy.

## How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open, then delete it.

- **Deleting your account** in My Circle removes your account, name, memberships, and notification tokens right away. If you set up a circle, the circle and all its check-ins and photos are deleted too. If you're the person checking in, your check-in photos are deleted.
- **Leaving or being removed from a circle** stops your access. Check-ins you already shared stay with the circle until it's deleted.
- **Backups** may keep copies for up to 30 days before they're overwritten.
- **Sign-in records** are kept for a limited time for security.

## Your choices and rights

- **See or correct your information.** Your name, circle, and settings are in the app. Email us for a copy of everything we hold about you.
- **Delete it.** Use Delete my account in My Circle, or email us.
- **Notifications.** You can turn them off in your phone's settings, but then you won't get reminders or alerts.
- **Camera.** DailyPulse asks before using the camera. You can say no and check in with a tap instead.

**State privacy laws.** Depending on where you live, including California, Minnesota, and other states, you may have rights to know, get a copy of, correct, or delete your information, and to not be discriminated against for asking. We don't sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. To make a request, email us. We'll confirm it's you and answer within 45 days. An authorized agent can make a request for you.

## Children, supported adults, and security

**Children.** DailyPulse is for people 18 and older. It isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If we learn a child under 13 has used it, we'll delete their information. If you think that's happened, email us.

**Adults supported by others.** When someone sets up DailyPulse for another adult, it's their responsibility to have that person's agreement, or the legal authority to agree for them. We don't verify this or keep records of it. A guardian or authorized representative can make privacy requests for the person they support.

**Security.** Information is encrypted on its way between your phone and our servers. Each circle's photos are stored privately and shown only to its members. Database rules stop anyone from seeing a circle they're not in, and we limit who at Condor can see your information. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.
