DailyPulse is a small hello each day between someone and the people who look out for them. If a check-in doesn't come, their circle hears about it.

## Get help

Email [support@condorllc.org](mailto:support@condorllc.org). Tell us what phone you use (iPhone or Android) and what happened, and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

**DailyPulse is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 911.**

## Common questions

**I'm not getting reminders or alerts.** Make sure notifications for DailyPulse are turned on in your phone's settings, and that the phone has signal or Wi-Fi. On Android, check that battery saving isn't stopping DailyPulse.

**How do I join a circle?** Get the free DailyPulse app, open it, tap "I have a join code," and enter the code the organizer sent you. The organizer then approves you.

**How do I cancel my subscription?** Subscriptions are paid through the App Store or Google Play, so cancel them there. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel it.

**I have a new phone.** Install DailyPulse and sign in with the same email. Your circle and subscription come with you. If the app says your circle isn't active, open Subscribe and tap "Already subscribed? Restore it."

**How do I leave a circle?** Open My Circle in the app and tap Leave the circle. The organizer can also remove people there.

## Delete your account

In the app, open My Circle and tap Delete my account. This deletes your account, name, memberships, and notification settings right away. If you set up a circle, the circle and all its check-ins and photos are deleted for everyone in it. If you're the person who checks in, your check-in photos are deleted.

If you can't open the app, email [support@condorllc.org](mailto:support@condorllc.org?subject=Delete%20my%20DailyPulse%20account) from the email you sign in with, or tell us the name on your account and the circle's name. We'll confirm it's you and delete it within 30 days. Backups are overwritten within 30 days after that.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first.
